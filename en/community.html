Why a subscription and not just a one-time product

Moving is a months-long process, and once you're settled, questions keep coming: your first tax return, renewing your permit, changing apartments, understanding a letter from your Gemeinde... The Community is built to support you not just through the move, but through your first years living in Switzerland.

What's included, on any plan

  • Private group — alongside other Spaniards moving or already settled, to solve day-to-day questions and make real connections.
  • Live monthly Q&A — an open session to ask whatever you need, when you need it.
  • Monthly newsletter with updated data — salaries and cost of living by sector and canton, more precise than the free quiz's fixed estimate.
  • Annual tax season help — a dedicated guide when Swiss tax season arrives, covering what's changed that year.

Which plan should you pick?

If you're not sure how long you'll need it, start with the monthly plan — no commitment. If you already know you'll be in the process for several months, or want to keep going after settling in, the 6-month or annual plans are much better value.

How it fits with the rest

The Community doesn't replace personalized consulting (that's an in-depth review of your specific case) or the Moving Kit (one-time templates and checklist). It's the complement for when you want ongoing company, not a one-off consultation.
