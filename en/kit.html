The Switzerland Moving Kit

Everything you need to write and organize, already done for you: a week-by-week checklist, your CV in Swiss format, cover letters in German and French, and the exact emails you'll actually need to send.

Why a paid kit if the guide is already free?

The free guide explains what you need to do and in what order. The Kit gives you the text already written: the CV template, translated and contextualized cover letters, and the exact emails you'll send to landlords, employers, and the administration — so you don't burn hours drafting from scratch in a language you may not fully master yet.

What's included

  • Week-by-week checklist — from 8 weeks before you travel through month 6, with each step at the right moment.
  • Swiss-format CV template — structure, what to include and avoid, with a filled-in example.
  • Cover letter in German — ready to adapt, with a reference translation into Spanish.
  • Cover letter in French — same logic, for French-speaking Switzerland.
  • Rental application dossier — document checklist plus sample email in German and French for apartment applications.
  • Extra sample emails — replying to a job offer, and requesting a municipal registration appointment.

Format

An 8-page PDF, delivered instantly after purchase. Built to copy, paste, and adapt directly — no special software needed.

What it isn't

This kit doesn't replace personalized consulting — it's self-service: you fill in the templates with your own information. If you'd rather we review your actual case with you, that's the right option.
