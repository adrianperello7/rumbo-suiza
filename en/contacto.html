1:1 consulting

Would you rather have us walk you through it, step by step?

The quiz gives you an estimate in 2 minutes. Personalized consulting goes further: we review your actual case, your CV and your documents, and build a concrete action plan with you.

What personalized consulting includes

  • Review of your CV, adapted to the Swiss market's format and expectations
  • Analysis of your specific situation: the right permit, exact deadlines and documents for your case
  • Realistic salary guidance based on your profile, sector and chosen canton
  • Follow-up on specific questions by video call or email, depending on the package chosen

Important

This consulting service is orientational and supportive — we are not a law firm or an official immigration agency. For complex legal matters (for example, dual nationality cases, criminal record issues, or particular family situations) we'll refer you to a licensed professional if your case requires it.

Get in touch

Tell us briefly about your situation (where you're starting from, what stage you're at, what you need) and we'll reply with next steps and the options available.

You can also write directly to info.rumbosuiza@gmail.com.
